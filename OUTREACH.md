## Purpose

Hu-Mind seeks staged help tied to falsifiable experiments rather than a general
request for “AGI hardware.” Near-term needs are:

1. technical review of efficient, isolated local inference;
2. independent evaluation and research-method guidance;
3. temporary compute for registered comparisons;
4. hardware support only after measured workload evidence; and
5. partners able to reproduce results and hold audit checkpoints.

## Readiness gaps

Before a formal funding or compute application, the project should produce:

- verified SIMBA-server hardware and runtime inventory;
- exact local model names, revisions, licences, quantization, and manifests;
- prompt, output, latency, VRAM, RAM, energy, and thermal measurements;
- a frozen small feasibility dataset and registered experiment plan;
- clear applicant status, including incorporation or academic sponsorship where
  required;
- a concise technical concept note and realistic budget;
- security controls for SIMBAX context selection and offline deployment;
- named independent evaluation and replication roles.

## Development-resourcing plan

The working estimate for a supervised research prototype is approximately
900,000 to 2,000,000 development tokens, with 1,500,000 used as a planning
midpoint rather than a commitment or measured requirement.

The intended allocation is hybrid:

- local SIMBAX/Qwen inference for bounded implementation, tests, documentation,
  and repetitive refactoring;
- stronger external models for architecture, difficult debugging, security
  review, and independent verification;
- human approval for scope, durable changes, external communication, and every
  consequential action.

Token counts are not yet measured. SIMBAX must retain Ollama prompt/evaluation
counts and durations before this estimate can support a funding case.

## Follow-up discipline

- Do not send repeated generic requests.
- Tailor every follow-up to the recipient's programme and eligibility rules.
- Attach or link only current, reviewed material.
- Record conflicts of interest, requested conditions, and resource restrictions.
- Do not offer exclusivity over safety findings or suppress negative results.
- Convert promising discussions into a named owner, next action, and date.
